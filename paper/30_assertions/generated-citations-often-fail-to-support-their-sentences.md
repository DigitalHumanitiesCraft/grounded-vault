# Generated citations frequently fail to support their sentences

## Statement

Across the audited generative search engines and their responses, only 51.5% of generated sentences are fully supported by their citations and only 74.5% of citations support the sentence they are attached to. Between systems the size of the shortfall varies widely, with citation recall running from 68.7 for the strongest system on that metric down to 11.1 for the weakest, and citation precision from 89.5 down to 63.6.

- [[20_distillates/documents/liu-2023-evaluating-verifiability-generative-search-engines#^s2]] — supplies the aggregate figures of 51.5% fully supported sentences and 74.5% supporting citations across all audited systems and responses.
- [[20_distillates/documents/liu-2023-evaluating-verifiability-generative-search-engines#^s14]] — supplies the per system spread of citation recall and citation precision that shows the shortfall is not confined to one product.
