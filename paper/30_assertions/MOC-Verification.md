# MOC: Verification

Assertions on the three checking instances and their separated authority, on the effect of pairing a producing model with a reviewer from another model family, and on the human bottleneck at the top rung of the status ladder.

## Open questions

- What pass rate of machine review justifies sampling instead of full human verification, and on what basis is that threshold set?
- How far do the error modes of two model families actually decorrelate, and does any run of the two instances allow that to be quantified?
