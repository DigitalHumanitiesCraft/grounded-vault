# MOC: Provenance

Assertions on the anchor relation itself, on what a machine-resolvable link to a source location guarantees and on the difference between provenance enforced by the form of the artifact and provenance reported by the producing system.

## Open questions

- Which provenance models outside this architecture enforce their links rather than record them, and what do they do at the layer boundaries this chain draws?
- What does an anchor guarantee where the source itself is generated material, and does the guarantee change when the chain reaches a generated ground?
