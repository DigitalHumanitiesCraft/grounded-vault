# The historical method separates origin checking from credibility assessment

## Statement

The classical handbooks of historical method split source criticism into two stages that are carried out in order. The first stage examines the material transmission of the document, its writing, its language, its forms and its sources, in order to restore the original wording and determine the provenance, and it decides whether the data are admissible as testimony at all. The second stage judges what the author meant, whether he believed what he said and whether he was entitled to believe it, and it weighs how the testimonies stand to the facts. The French handbook names the two stages critique externe and critique interne (Langlois and Seignobos 1898). The German handbook names them outer and inner criticism and makes the ordering explicit by requiring the result of the first stage to be established before the second proceeds (Bernheim 1908).

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/langlois-seignobos-1898-introduction#^s1]], assigns the examination of writing, language, forms and sources, with the restitution of the wording and the determination of provenance, to the critique externe.
- [[20_distillates/publications/langlois-seignobos-1898-introduction#^s2]], assigns the questions of meaning, belief and entitlement to belief to the critique interne, which follows the external stage.
- [[20_distillates/publications/bernheim-1908-lehrbuch-historische-methode#^s2]], supplies the German pair of outer criticism, judging admissibility as testimony, and inner criticism, judging how the testimonies stand to the facts.
- [[20_distillates/publications/bernheim-1908-lehrbuch-historische-methode#^s7]], states that inner criticism takes up the results of outer criticism, which fixes the order of the two stages.
