# MOC: Agentic Workflow

How agents produce and check the structure. It covers the separation of production and checking, the operator's place in the loop, and the design choices of the machine review. Supporting strands hold context engineering as the practice the method instantiates and Promptotyping as the method of which Grounded Vault is a profile.

- [[30_assertions/agentic-context-engineering-as-design-task]], the model vendor's engineering post as accessed on 2026-08-10 supplies definition and criterion for context assembly, the position experiments supply its empirical subject matter.
- [[30_assertions/machine-review-at-c726eb5-judges-a-bare-statement-against-its-anchored-location]], at commit c726eb5, what enters a review pair and what the reviewer is kept from seeing.
- [[30_assertions/agentic-promptotyping-knowledge-base-artifact]], the method paper's review draft of 2026-07-31 organizes Promptotyping work around a maintained, versioned project knowledge base.
