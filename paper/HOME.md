# Grounded Vault internal method manuscript

This instance contains one English manuscript that develops the Grounded Vault method through the evidence chain the method prescribes. The manuscript is an internal research artefact and has no submission target. The Promptotyping paper remains a separate publication project.

## Read the manuscript

- [[40_output/grounded-vault-method]] presents the support gap, the layer architecture, the checking model, the recorded review findings, and the limits of the method.

## Follow the evidence chain

- [[30_assertions/MOC-Provenance]] covers traceability and its epistemic limits.
- [[30_assertions/MOC-Verification]] covers attribution, review, and human verification.
- [[30_assertions/MOC-Architecture]] covers layers, source types, anchor forms, and portability.
- [[30_assertions/MOC-Agentic-Workflow]] covers context assembly and the maintained knowledge base.
- [[30_assertions/MOC-Instances]] covers the recorded review run and its defect categories.

## Understand the instance

- [[knowledge/specification]] records purpose, parameters, and the single manuscript decision.
- [[knowledge/state]] records the source inventory and manuscript status.
- [[knowledge/journal]] records provenance and decisions.
- The root `knowledge/` directory defines the shared schema and operations.

## Status vocabulary

`grounded` records a produced anchor structure. `validated` additionally records deterministic validation and adversarial machine review. `verified` records human expert verification. `contested` preserves an unresolved source conflict.
