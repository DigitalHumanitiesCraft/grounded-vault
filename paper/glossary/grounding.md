# Grounding

At commit `79cd1d3`, the Grounded Vault terminology defines grounding as the anchor relation between an assertion and its source locations, a structural property an agent can produce that says nothing about whether the statement is true. [[10_markdown/documents/grounded-vault-index-79cd1d3#^idx18]] Its counterpart on the judgment side is [[glossary/evidence]].
