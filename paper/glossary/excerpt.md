# Excerpt

The humanities working practice of writing out selected passages of a source, verbatim or paraphrased, each with a precise locator and kept separate from the reader's own commentary. The excerpt is the manual predecessor of the [[glossary/distillate]], one source, selected statements, each anchored to its location.
