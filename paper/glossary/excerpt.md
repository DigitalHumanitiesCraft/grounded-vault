# Excerpt

The humanities working practice of writing out selected passages of a source, verbatim or paraphrased, each with a precise locator and kept separate from the reader's own commentary. This vault reads the excerpt as a manual counterpart of the [[glossary/distillate]], because both bind what they take from one source to its location.
